# Learning outcome

## RA1

**Recognizes the structure of data networks by identifying their components and operating principles.**

U01 introduces and develops this learning outcome. Its purpose is to build the conceptual and technical map students need in order to understand a network before moving into detailed administration.

RA1 is **not completed in U01**. U02 — Layers, Encapsulation, and Analysis especially completes the formal development of network architectures, layers, protocols, and protocol stacks.

---

# RA1 assessment criteria

| CE | Assessment criterion |
|---|---|
| **RA1.a** | The factors driving the continuous expansion and evolution of data networks have been identified. |
| **RA1.b** | The different transmission media used in networks have been distinguished. |
| **RA1.c** | The different types of networks and their topologies have been recognized. |
| **RA1.d** | Network architectures and the layers that make them up have been described. |
| **RA1.e** | The concept of a communication protocol has been described. |
| **RA1.f** | The operation of protocol stacks in different network architectures has been described. |
| **RA1.g** | The functional, physical, and logical components of data networks have been presented and described. |
| **RA1.h** | Network interconnection devices have been distinguished according to the functional layer in which they operate. |

# How the assessment criteria are addressed in U01

U01 does not attempt to develop every RA1 assessment criterion to the same depth.

| CE | Treatment in U01 | Continuity |
|---|---|---|
| **RA1.a** | **Main development** | Later integrated into the study of services, availability, and network evolution. |
| **RA1.b** | **Initial introduction and distinction** of copper, fiber, and radio | Transmission media are studied technically in U03. |
| **RA1.c** | **Main development** | Reused throughout the module. |
| **RA1.d** | **Introduction** through service architectures and the first layered model | Formal development in U02. |
| **RA1.e** | **Initial development** of the protocol concept and the need for shared rules | Further development and practical observation in U02. |
| **RA1.f** | **Minimal preview** | Formal development in U02 through TCP/IP, encapsulation, PDUs, and analysis. |
| **RA1.g** | **Main development** | Later made more concrete through infrastructure, addressing, and device administration. |
| **RA1.h** | **Initial introduction and application** | Expanded through layered models and later device administration. |

U01 especially prioritizes **RA1.a, RA1.b, RA1.c, RA1.g, and RA1.h**.  
Criteria **RA1.d, RA1.e, and RA1.f** appear as part of the initial conceptual map and are developed in greater depth in U02.

# Relationship between U01 and later units

```text
U01 — Network Fundamentals and Architecture
        ↓
U02 — Layers, Encapsulation, and Analysis
        ↓
U03 — Infrastructure and Connectivity
        ↓
U04 — IPv4, Subnetting, VLSM, CIDR, and DHCP
        ↓
U05 — IPv6 and Autoconfiguration
        ↓
U06–U10 — Switching, VLANs, Routing, WAN, Internet, and NAT
```

The instructional boundaries are intentional:

- U01 **recognizes** transmission media; U03 studies them technically.
- U01 presents layers as an initial map; U02 formally develops models, protocols, encapsulation, and analysis.
- U01 introduces IP, MAC, ports, names, and the gateway; U04 and U05 develop addressing.
- U01 recognizes switches, routers, and access points by their general function; later units develop their administration.
- U01 introduces evidence-based observation and troubleshooting; the process is reused and expanded throughout the module.

# What students should understand about RA, CE, and U01

- An **RA (learning outcome)** describes the overall performance students are expected to achieve.
- A **CE (assessment criterion)** specifies evidence used to assess that learning outcome.
- **U01** contributes to RA1 but **does not complete it by itself**.
- Content is sequenced to support learning, even when the administrative organization of assessment criteria does not exactly match the instructional order.
- U02 continues and completes RA1 before its specific assessment.
